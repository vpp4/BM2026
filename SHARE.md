Subject: Offline event finder for the burn — one file, works with no signal

Attached is a search tool for everything happening at BRC this year:
3,408 events and the full 1,604-slot DJ lineup. It runs entirely on your
phone with no signal, no app, no account.

Save it BEFORE you leave — it can't download itself out there.

iPhone
  1. Tap the attachment → Share icon → Save to Files
  2. Open the Files app and tap playabrain.html (opens in Safari)
  3. That's it. Works in airplane mode forever after.

Android
  1. Download the attachment
  2. Open it from your Downloads (choose Chrome if asked)

What it does
  • Search every event, camp, and DJ by name or description
  • "on now" shows what's running near you right now
  • Walk times from OUR camp are already built in — no setup
  • Tap ★ to save things; your list survives closing the browser
  • "♪ Sets" switches from talks to the DJ lineup
  • Filters → hours, walk distance, and 16 interest tags

Notes
  • Times are what camps submitted in advance. They will drift. Treat them
    as intentions, not promises.
  • The DJ lineup is pre-release, so expect changes.
  • Nothing is tracked and nothing is sent anywhere. It's one HTML file.
